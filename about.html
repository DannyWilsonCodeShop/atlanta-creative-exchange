About ACE

The story behind the sound, the art, and the community.

It Started with a Simple Belief

That musicians, artists, and moment makers in all industries deserve a platform to make their dreams a reality.

Atlanta Creative Exchange was born in 2021 from a founder's desire to create something that didn't exist yet — a space where creative people could find the support they need to bring their visions to life. Not just a vendor, not just a service. A community. A launchpad. A home base for people who make things.

We saw musicians with incredible talent who just needed a stage, a sound system, and someone in their corner. We saw artists looking for a community that understood them. We saw event planners who wanted more than a transaction — they wanted a creative partner.

So we built one. Atlanta Creative Exchange exists to promote live performance and presentation, to support creative adventures in all forms, and to prove that when you give people the right platform, extraordinary things happen.

What Drives Us

Create.

We exist to give people the tools, the stage, and the technology to make what's inside them real. Your potential deserves a platform — we build it.

Moments.

A live performance that gives someone chills. An event that people talk about for years. A song that changes someone's day. That's what we're after.

Exchange.

Ideas flow when people connect. We create spaces — physical and digital — where artists, musicians, technologists, and dreamers meet and make things happen together.

Love.

This isn't a corporate play. Everything we do comes from genuine love for music, for art, for community, and for the people brave enough to share their work with the world.

Making dreams a reality, one moment at a time.

Rooted in Atlanta

Atlanta has always been a city that creates culture. From hip-hop to visual arts, from tech startups to live theater — this city breeds creative people. ACE is built for them.

We serve musicians, artists, event planners, entrepreneurs, and anyone with a creative vision that needs support. Whether you're planning your first open mic or your hundredth corporate event, we meet you where you are.

This is more than a business. It's a commitment to keeping Atlanta's creative pulse alive and making sure every dreamer has a shot at bringing their thing to life.
