About ACE

Founded in 2021, Atlanta Creative Exchange grew from a simple belief: communities thrive when people have space to create, perform, and connect through art.

Rooted in Atlanta's East Lake neighborhood, ACE provides DJ services, PA system rentals, and live band coordination for events across the city. But we're more than a service provider — we're a creative community.

Through our Creative Rest event series, we bring together local artists, musicians, and creatives for nights of live music, visual art, and genuine human connection. No pretense, just expression.
