Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a website or app take to build?

Landing pages: 1–2 weeks. Multi-page websites: 3–6 weeks. Web applications: 6–12 weeks. Mobile apps: 8–16 weeks. Timelines depend on complexity, feedback speed, and scope — we'll give you a specific timeline with your quote.

Do I own the code and the app after it's built?

Yes. You own all code, designs, and content we create for you. We hand over everything and help you get set up on your own cloud accounts. Optional ongoing maintenance is available if you'd like us to keep running it.

What are the payment terms?

Most projects run on a deposit to start plus milestone payments as work is delivered. We'll lay out the exact schedule in your quote so there are no surprises.

Can you build on top of our existing app or site?

Usually, yes. We can add features, modernize, or rebuild on a stronger foundation. We'll review what you have and recommend the most cost-effective path.

Why AWS?

AWS lets us build serverless apps that cost very little while you're small and scale automatically as you grow — no servers to babysit, security handled by default. Enterprise-grade infrastructure on a startup budget.

Do you offer ongoing support after launch?

Yes. We offer monthly maintenance and support retainers for monitoring, updates, and new features — or you can take full ownership and we're here when you need us.

What does it cost to run the app each month?

Serverless hosting for a small-to-mid app is typically low — often $50–$200/month depending on traffic and features. We'll estimate your running costs up front.

Do you work with clients outside Atlanta?

Yes. We're based in Atlanta and available on-site locally, and we work with clients remotely anywhere in the US.
