Frequently Asked Questions

Does the DJ price include speakers and sound?

Yes. Every DJ booking includes a basic PA system sized appropriately for your venue. For larger spaces or outdoor events that need additional coverage, an upgraded PA add-on may apply — we'll let you know when we quote.

What's included in a PA System rental?

Speakers, stands, mixer, and cabling appropriate for your space. Delivery, setup, and breakdown are included. Microphones, monitors, and a sound technician are add-ons based on your needs.

How long is a standard DJ set?

Our flat rates cover a standard 4-hour performance window. Need more time? Overtime is available at $150–$200/hr.

What are the payment terms?

A deposit is required to secure your date. The remaining balance is due within 24 hours of event completion. We'll provide specific payment details and the deposit amount with your personalized quote.

Do you travel outside Atlanta?

Yes! We serve the greater Atlanta metro area and can travel beyond for an additional travel fee. Let us know your venue location and we'll include any travel costs in your quote.

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend booking at least 2–4 weeks in advance for most events. Weddings and large events should book 2–3 months out to ensure availability. Last-minute requests are welcome — we'll do our best to accommodate.

Can I combine DJ and live band at the same event?

Absolutely. Many events feature a live band for the ceremony or cocktail hour and a DJ for the reception/party. Both services are quoted at their respective rates with applicable bundle discounts.

What if I need to cancel or reschedule?

We understand plans change. Rescheduling is free with 2+ weeks notice. Cancellation terms and deposit refund details will be outlined in your booking agreement.

Do you provide lighting?

We focus on sound, music, and event coordination. Lighting can be arranged through our network of partners — let us know what you need and we'll connect you or include it in a custom package.
